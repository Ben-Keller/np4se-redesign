# NP4SE logo

The mark is a gold sun, a little more than half risen, cut by fine gaps that widen as it sinks towards a navy horizon line.

| Folder | What's in it | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `logo/svg` | The mark in three drawings (`np4se-mark`, `-compact`, `-small`), each in colour, reversed, navy and white | Anything digital or printed. SVG stays sharp at any size. |
| `logo/png` | The full mark at 600 and 2000 px wide, on a transparent background | Word, PowerPoint and anywhere that won't take SVG |
| `icons` | Favicons (SVG, ICO, 16 and 32 px), the Apple touch icon, 192 and 512 px app icons and an 800 px social avatar | Websites, phone home screens and social media profiles |

The three drawings are the same sun cut differently, so the stripes stay clear at every size. Use the full drawing from 48 px tall upwards (in print, whenever the mark is more than about 2 cm wide), the compact one between 24 and 48 px, and the small one below that. The reversed version, with a lighter gold sun and a white horizon, is for navy backgrounds and dark photos. The navy and white versions are for one-colour printing, embossing and busy images. Leave clear space of at least a sixth of the mark's width all around it.

The colours are navy `#0F2B3D` (RGB 15 43 61), gold `#DDA12E` (RGB 221 161 46), and light gold `#E9B54A` (RGB 233 181 74) for the sun on dark backgrounds.

Next to the mark, the name is set in Hanken Grotesk SemiBold capitals with letter-spacing of 0.17 em, over two lines: "New Producers for / Sustainable Energy". The short form uses NP4SE in Source Serif 4 SemiBold. Both typefaces are free from Google Fonts. The logo canvas shows the lockups, clear space and colour versions together.
