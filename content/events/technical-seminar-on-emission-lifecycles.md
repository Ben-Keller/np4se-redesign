<p>This virtual seminar will convene the technical officers of the five delegations participating in the <a href="/event/near-zero-methane-and-zero-flaring-supporting-tangible-action-in-emerging-producers/">2023 NPG training on emissions</a>. Technical officers from other member countries may also attend.</p>
<p>Our speakers, TJ Conway, RMI and Deborah Gordon, RMI, will present the OPGEE model and illustrate its application with proxy fields.</p>
<p>Objectives: Familiarize participants with assessment of lifecycle emissions. Identify critical variables for modelling lifecycle emissions of key fields.</p>
<p>Process:</p>
<ul>
<li>Before the technical seminar: Each team identifies 2 target projects the country will work on. Each country provides slides by 4 September of their target projects and what they know or expect about their emissions profile.</li>
<li>NPG team identifies relevant proxies</li>
<li>Technical seminar: Demonstration of modelling and key variables at technical seminar. NPG team identifies a list of variables and data the technical team should compile</li>
<li>Technical teams work on developing lifecycle projection of emissions and identifying levers to reduce emissions</li>
<li>Technical teams present their progress at the workshop/training</li>
</ul>
