<p>This meeting was part of the series on Fostering Resilience in Crisis and focused on how countries can optimise value creation through competitive bidding. The meeting featured Juan Carlos Boue,  <a href="https://www.curtis.com/our-people/juan-carlos-boue/experience" rel="noopener">Legal Counsel</a> at Curtis &amp; Co and <a href="https://www.oxfordenergy.org/authors/juan-carlos-boue/" rel="noopener">Research Associate</a> at Oxford Institute for Energy Studies, and debated the value of the pure cash bonus bidding mechanism – in contrast to work programme bidding and mixed bidding, highlighting that cash bonus bidding increases competition and gives better prices for acreage. The full recording is available on <a href="https://forum.newproducersgroup.org" rel="noopener">the members’ platform.</a></p>
