<p>This five-day course couples traditional transparency and accountability guidance with advanced communication strategy skills. Using case studies of resource rich countries that have gotten communication “right” and “wrong,” this course helps government officials and civil society leaders build an understanding of how communication is linked with good governance and what tools of communication and transparency they can use to build systems of accountability.</p>
