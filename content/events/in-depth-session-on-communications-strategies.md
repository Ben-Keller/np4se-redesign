<p>Thanks to executive and communications coaches, Sheldon Daniel and Donna Ramsammy, we ran this session of the Fostering Resilience in Crisis series like a communication clinic. We heard the communication challenge of three different organisations (and people within these organisations) and in a back and forth, Sheldon and Donna clarified the context and offered some strategies for tackling these challenges. This meeting was off the record and the recording is available on <a href="https://forum.newproducersgroup.org" rel="noopener">the members’ platform</a></p>
