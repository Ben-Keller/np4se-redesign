<p>In this first meeting of a series focused on how national oil companies can add value in the non-operator role, we will hear from two seasoned industry executives, who will discuss what they learned in interfacing with oil company partners.</p>
<p>Amalia Olivera Riley and John Farrelly will focus on the following, illustrating with case studies:</p>
<p>1) Building the right partnerships (assessing industry partners for optimal value creation)</p>
<p>2) Adding value as a non operator through simple steps</p>
<p>3) Evolving the relationship with basin maturity (right partners for the right time)</p>
<p>4) Positioning as a key participant in the JV</p>
<p>NOCs in the New Producers for Sustainable Energy are broadly non-operators. There are opportunities in that role to guide decisions to protect the State’s priorities and interests.</p>
<p>Interested NOC executives and government regulators are invited to join the virtual seminar at 2pm (London). <a href="/contact-us/">Express interest</a> in participating.</p>
