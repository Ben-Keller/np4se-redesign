<p>Methane is the second largest greenhouse gas with a global warming effect over 80 times greater than CO2. More than 30% of the current rise in temperatures is due to this potent gas. To secure global net zero by 2050 and keep 1.5 degrees within reach, urgent reductions<br/>
are needed.</p>
<p>Governments have a critical role to play in making this a reality. This high-level side event will be a critical dialogue for Commonwealth countries and partners to:<br/>
• Explore areas where greater support and cooperation is needed;<br/>
• Identify opportunities for collaboration, including a proposed action group under the Commonwealth Sustainable Energy Transitions (CSET) Agenda</p>
<p>Speakers:</p>
<p>Rt Hon Patricia Scotland QC, Secretary-General of the Commonwealth<br/>
Hon Min Stuart Richard Young, Minister, Energy and Energy Industries, Trinidad and Tobago<br/>
Naadira Ogeer, Economic Advisor, TONR, Commonwealth Secretariat<br/>
Giulia Ferrini, Programme Officer, UN Environment Programme<br/>
Chair: Dr Ruth Kattumuri, Senior Director, EYSD, Commonwealth Secretariat</p>
