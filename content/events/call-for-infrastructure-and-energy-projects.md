<p>The Africa Infrastructure Development Association <a href="https://afida-africa.org/" rel="noopener">AfIDA</a> is offering our African member countries eight exclusive Catapult Pitch Sessions. Organisations in our member countries in Africa can select national infrastructure and energy projects to showcase to a panel of major private and public investors in Accra from 15-19 September 2025, during Africa Oil Week. Projects can include green or blue hydrogen, petroleum storage and pipelines, gas-to-power, geothermal, and much more.</p>
<p>Interested governments and their partners can <a href="https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/AfIDA_SAI2025" rel="noopener">complete the Project Pitch Survey</a> to describe their project ideas (or contact us to discuss ideas). AfIDA will provide support in the months preceding the Catapult Pitch to define and develop project ideas that are deemed suitable.</p>
<p>AfIDA will also offer a half-day training on Project Development &amp; Finance to help structure investment-ready proposals. To express interest in participating in the training or to support it with funding <a href="/contact-us/">contact us.</a></p>
