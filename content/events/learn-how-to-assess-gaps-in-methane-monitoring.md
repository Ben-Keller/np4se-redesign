<h3><img alt="" height="108" loading="lazy" src="/assets/img/wp/2025-01-screenshot-2025-01-20-at-3-04-15-pm.webp" width="252"/>Demonstration of the Mist Tool</h3>
<p>New rules that have emerged in the EU, Korea and Japan will require exporters to measure the methane intensity of their cargo. In this interactive seminar, Valerie Marcel of the New Producers introduced the new methane import rules and Carbon Limits offered a demonstration of the <a href="https://www.mist.carbonlimits.no/" rel="noopener">Mist tool</a>, which enables national oil companies to calculate their (current/expected) methane emissions and assess what level of measurement, monitoring, reporting and validation (MMRV) they are at.</p>
<p>Governments and NOCs can <strong><a href="https://forum.newproducersgroup.org/t/important-new-methane-rules/777/5?u=vmarcel" rel="noopener">watch the recording</a></strong> on the members’ platform.</p>
<p>Find out more about these new import rules for oil and gas and what the New Producers for Sustainable plans to do to help our member countries prepare.</p>
