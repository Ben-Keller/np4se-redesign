<p>The prospect of a transition away from fossil fuels creates many uncertainties for the petroleum industry. In the immediate context, the economic impact of the coronavirus pandemic has triggered an oil price collapse. This changing context may call for new approaches to petroleum contracts. Pedro Van Meurs shared his thoughts, based on his <a href="https://www.newproducersgroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/World-Petroleum-Industry-Perspectives_VanMeurs.pdf">extensive and well researched presentation</a>. This meeting was under Chatham House Rule and the recording is available on <a href="https://forum.newproducersgroup.org" rel="noopener">the members’ platform</a>.</p>
