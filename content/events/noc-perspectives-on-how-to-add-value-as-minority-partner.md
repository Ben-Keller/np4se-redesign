<p>Our first seminar on how <a href="/event/adding-value-as-non-operator-noc/">NOCs can add value as non-operators</a> was led by experienced international oil company executives, who shared how they maximised asset value as minority partners in joint ventures. The second seminar will give the floor to experienced NOC executives, who will discuss how they have succeeded in getting the State’s interests and policy objectives  better prioritised by the IOC operators while minority equity partners in projects.</p>
<p>This seminar will be virtual and members of the New Producers for Sustainable Energy and established NOCs can <a href="/contact-us/">reach out</a> to indicate their interest.  Dates are to be confirmed.</p>
