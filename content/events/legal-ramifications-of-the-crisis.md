<p>This meeting in the series on Fostering Resilience in Crisis examined the challenges that new producers face in managing their contracts and relationships with contractors during the current economic crisis. The discussion focused on the potential application of force majeure provisions and how governments can make decisions about disruptions to contracts and operations that help navigate the challenges of the current moment with an eye to the future. This meeting was off the record.</p>
