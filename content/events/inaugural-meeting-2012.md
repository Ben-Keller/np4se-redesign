<p>The inaugural meeting of the New Producers Group asked what challenges are faced by new producers and producers seeking to restructure their governance set up? Are these unique to emerging producers? Discussions focused on getting a good deal in licensing and reforming the institutional set up to oversee the sector. This meeting was held in London and discussions were under Chatham House Rule. Download the paper that emerged from the meeting “<a href="https://www.newproducersgroup.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Governance-Challenges-for-Emerging-Oil-and-Gas-Producers-background-paper.pdf">Governance Challenges for Emerging Oil and Gas Producers</a>”.</p>
