<p>The roundtable convened in London, bringing together government and NOC representatives from São Tomé and Príncipe, India, the Gambia, and Nigeria to discuss approaches to licensing. Keith Myers of Westwood Energy Group and Armando Zamora of the NP4SE and former head of the ANH of Colombia presented on how States can maximise national benefits in each stage of exploration from frontier to emerging to mature areas.</p>
