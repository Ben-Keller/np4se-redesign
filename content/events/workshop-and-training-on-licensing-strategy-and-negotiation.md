<p>The New Producers for Sustainable Energy and the Petroleum Commission, Ghana are organising a <strong>workshop and training on licensing and negotiation on 7-8 November in Takoradi, Ghana</strong>. The main purpose of the event is to review licensing strategies in today’s context and ask where flexibility on terms is warranted, and where it will harm long-term State interests. These discussions will help us update our <a href="/guidelines-for-good-governance-in-emerging-oil-and-gas-producers/">2016 Guidelines for Good Governance</a>, which were developed in a very different market and policy context.</p>
<p>The Workshop sessions 7-8 November will cover the following themes:</p>
<ol>
<li>Global trends in investment and fiscal terms</li>
<li>Country experience: Protecting State interests in licensing</li>
<li>Getting the process right</li>
<li>Reviewing licensing priorities</li>
</ol>
<p>The <strong>training on negotiation</strong> will be in the afternoon of 8 November and cover the following:</p>
<ol>
<li>What is negotiable given the legislative framework?</li>
<li>Preparing for negotiation</li>
<li>Principles</li>
<li>Mock negotiation</li>
</ol>
<p>Participants are invited to attend Ghana’s Local Content Conference, which takes place 4-6 November in Takoradi.</p>
<p>Invitations have been sent to all member countries. <a href="/contact-us/">Reach out</a> to the secretariat to express your interest in attending.</p>
