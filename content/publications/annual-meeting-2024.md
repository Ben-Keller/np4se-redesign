<h2>2024 Annual Meeting: Summary and analysis</h2>
<p>Paramaribo, 3-4 June 2024</p>
<p>Staatsolie hosted the 2024 Annual meeting in Paramaribo, Suriname. It brought together governments, NOCs, industry partners, and technical experts for a two-day international discussion, as well as training.</p>
<h2>Key messages</h2>
<ul>
<li>The New Producers for Sustainable Energy 2024 annual meeting addressed the challenges and opportunities faced by emerging producers in developing natural gas resources and managing greenhouse gas emissions.</li>
<li>The global investment mood in oil and gas is bullish, but capital expenditure is unevenly distributed, favouring North America and Latin America.</li>
<li>Equity issues are central for member countries, with the Global North discouraging new oil and gas projects in the Global South, while increasing their own production.</li>
<li>The Global South members of the NP4SE emphasised the need for energy access to alleviate poverty and drive development.</li>
<li>Lock-in risks associated with gas infrastructure investments are lower for downstream projects and can be mitigated for upstream projects by lowering costs and execution time, notably through small-scale and modular approaches.</li>
<li>Monetizing associated gas and methane presents challenges due to immature markets and infrastructure but offers significant economic and environmental benefits.</li>
<li>Growing focus on GHG emissions has led to increased pressure on operators to reduce emissions, particularly methane.</li>
<li>Financing decarbonization and gas projects remains a challenge, with limited public finance available and commercial banks hesitant to invest.</li>
<li>New producers are exploring alternative financing options, such as climate bonds and partnerships with Asian importing countries and institutions like the African Energy Bank.</li>
</ul>
